Shadow Ninja Survival – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy applies to the mobile game Shadow Ninja Survival (package: com.alen.shadowninjasurvival) developed by Alen.

Data collection

The game does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no internet permission and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (gold, unlocked stages, stars, settings) is stored only in the app's folder on your device and is never sent to any server. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If future versions add features such as advertising, this policy will be updated on this page.
